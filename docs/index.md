# SpeedPainter: AI Whiteboard Video Maker & Sketch Drawing Animation Studio

Welcome to the official technical and developer documentation for **[SpeedPainter (https://www.speedpainter.org/en)](https://www.speedpainter.org/en)** — the neural vector stroke trajectory synthesizer, AI speedpaint generator, and narrated whiteboard explainer video maker.

## Official Web Studios & Live Endpoints

- **[SpeedPainter Official Studio (AI Speedpaint & Sketch Animation Generator)](https://www.speedpainter.org/en)** — Convert static images, portraits, diagrams, and illustrations into realistic hand-drawn sketch animations (MP4 & GIF).
- **[AI Whiteboard & Explainer Video Maker (Script-to-Video)](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video)** — Turn plain-text scripts, Markdown docs, and voiceovers into multi-scene narrated whiteboard explainer videos.
- **[AI Drawing Timelapse Generator](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/ai-drawing-timelapse-generator)** — Turn photos and artwork into realistic step-by-step hand-drawn speedpaint timelapse videos.
- **[Whiteboard Video Maker](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/whiteboard-video-maker)** — Build animated whiteboard explainer presentations from text outlines and lesson notes.
- **[Doodle Video Maker](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/doodle-video-maker)** — Create hand-drawn doodle clips for product demos, tutorials, and short-form social channels.
- **[Explainer Video Agent Skill & MCP Guide](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/skills/explainer-video)** — Connect Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor directly to the SpeedPainter MCP server.
